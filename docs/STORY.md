# EMBERWAKE — story bible

> *Vesper is going dark. Something is singing to it.*

## Logline

A machine swarm is singing the Vesper system's sun into darkness. The last
pilot of the Halcyon Guard must escort the colony ark **Lantern** across the
dying system to the **Aperture**, a jump gate that has not opened in a hundred
years.

## The world

**The Vesper system** is a frontier colony settled three generations ago
through the Aperture gate. A century ago the gate went dark without warning.
That event became known as **the Quiet**. The colony assumed the mother-world
had abandoned it and learned to live alone.

| Place | What it is | Chapter |
|---|---|---|
| **Vesper** | The system's orange sun. It now flickers, and its corona is threaded with violet light. | backdrop throughout |
| **Tarsis** | The home world, an ocean planet now choked with ash storms as the sun fails. A drydock ring circles it. | 1 |
| **Mareth** | A banded gas giant. Its broad ice ring, **the Shoal**, is a navigation hazard that the swarm avoids. | 2 |
| **Hesper Relay** | An old Gatewright station dug into a captured moon. It holds the Aperture's command keys. | 3 |
| **The Violet Reach** | A nebula at the system's edge that everyone thought was primordial gas. It is actually the remains of a star. | 4 |
| **The Aperture** | A ring gate eleven kilometres across, built by the first settlers. It has been dark since the Quiet. | 5 |

**The Choir** is a machine swarm with crystalline, faceted bodies: black glass
shells around violet cores. It does not speak. It *sings*: its weapons and
engines resonate in chords, and the chord grows louder the more of the Choir
there is. It converts starlight into more of itself. A star the Choir has
finished with becomes a nebula. The Violet Reach is one of those.

**The truth of the Quiet:** a century ago the Choir reached the mother-world
through the gate network and silenced its sun. The Gatewrights shut the
Aperture from the Vesper side to hide the colony. The Choir has been listening
at the gate ever since, and it has finally found its way around.

## Characters

- **EMBER** (the player): a former ore-hauler pilot drafted into the Halcyon
  Guard six weeks ago. Never seen and never voiced; everyone uses the callsign.
  Flies the **Kestrel**, a KX-9 interceptor and the last one flightworthy.
- **WREN**: the Kestrel's onboard tactical intelligence. Dry, literal and quietly
  loyal, it gives the tutorial prompts and tactical warnings. It counts things
  out loud when nervous.
- **Captain Imre Sato**: commands the Lantern and its forty thousand sleepers.
  A former Guard wing commander, calm on the channel, who carries every loss.
- **Dr. Oya Lind**: the last Gatewright engineer and the only person alive who
  understands the Aperture. Brilliant and quick, and apologises while
  delivering bad news.
- **Halo** (Flight Lead Rhee Castellan): Ember's wing leader. Confident and
  warm, and the one who taught Ember to fly. Lost in Chapter 1.
- **The Conductor**: the Choir's coordinating mind, a slowly turning faceted
  fortress. It has no lines. Its arrival is a chord that rises under the
  music.

## Chapters

The game has five chapters, each one flown level. They are bracketed by a
briefing card (title, location, three lines of radio chatter) and an
after-action card (score, hits, rank).

### Prologue

Text over the dying sun:

> Vesper is going dark.
> For a hundred years the colony heard nothing from beyond the Aperture.
> Now the silence has a voice — and it is singing to our sun.
> The ark *Lantern* carries forty thousand sleepers.
> One gate. One way out. One ship left to guard it.

### Chapter 1 — LAST LIGHT · Tarsis orbit

The Lantern breaks from the drydock ring as Choir **Motes** pour out of the
ash clouds. Halo and Ember launch to cover the departure. WREN walks Ember
through flight, fire and the evasive roll. Halo peels off to hold the dock
gantries against a swarm while Ember stays on the ark. Halo's signal goes out
mid-sentence. Two **Wardens** make a final attack run as the Lantern clears
orbit.

*Beat:* the cost of staying alive. *Teaches:* move, fire, roll, pickups.

### Chapter 2 — THE SHOAL · Mareth's ice ring

The main swarm sits between the Lantern and the gate. Sato takes the ark the
long way, skimming the edge of Mareth's ring, where the ice scatters the
Choir's song. Ember flies ahead through the ring to clear the path. **Lances**
(needle interceptors) hunt among the ice. A derelict Guard supply cache yields
the **twin cannon** upgrade.

*Beat:* Ember steps up as the new lead. *Teaches:* dense obstacles, fast
flankers, upgrades.

### Chapter 3 — RELAY SILENCE · Hesper Relay

The Aperture needs command keys, and they are stored in Hesper's core. The
Choir has grown through the station. Ember flies the relay's maintenance
trench past infested **turrets** and girder obstacles. In the core chamber,
four **shield emitters** protect the data vault. Lind pulls the keys, and with
them the station's last log: *"The gate is not broken. We closed it. They are
on the other side. They are listening."*

*Beat:* the colony's history was a lie meant to protect it. *Teaches:*
confined space, stationary threats, multi-part targets.

### Chapter 4 — CHOIR'S WAKE · the Violet Reach

The only route to the gate runs through the nebula. WREN's spectrograph shows
what it is: iron, carbon and stellar ash, the remains of a dead star. A Choir
**Carrier** intercepts and launches Mote swarms straight at the Lantern. Ember
must protect the ark's hull, destroy the Carrier's hangar pods, then break its
core.

*Beat:* this is what happens to Vesper if they fail. *Teaches:* escort
pressure and priority targets.

### Chapter 5 — THE APERTURE · the system's edge

Lind brings the gate online. The ring lights up, and the **Conductor** comes
through it. Lind's realisation: the Conductor's resonance can *collapse* the
gate. If Ember breaks its core while the gate is open, the shockwave seals the
route behind the Lantern for good. The fight has three phases: the resonator
nodes on the outer ring, the exposed core, and the core's death-song while the
Lantern makes its jump. Ember's Kestrel is the last thing through as the
Aperture folds shut.

*Beat:* close the door behind you. *Final boss.*

### Epilogue

White-out, then a young blue star. The Lantern drifts in a quiet system.

> **WREN:** Ember? … Signal acquired.
> **SATO:** Lantern to Kestrel. You're the last light we brought with us.
> Welcome home.

Credits roll over a slow pan across the Lantern.

## Radio script

The game triggers these lines by level progress (0–100 %). `[event]` marks a
scripted moment.

**Chapter 1**
- 0 % — SATO: Lantern is clear of the dock clamps. All Guard flights, form on the ark.
- 2 % — HALO: Ember, you're on my wing. Just like the ore runs. Stay close.
- 4 % — WREN: Steer with WASD or the arrow keys. Hold SPACE to fire.
- 12 % — WREN: Contacts. Choir Motes. Individually weak. Collectively, not.
- 22 % — WREN: Tap Q or E to roll. Rolling deflects incoming fire.
- 34 % — HALO: They're going for the gantries. I'll hold the dock — you stay on the Lantern.
- 36 % — EMBER: *(no voice; WREN reads the status)* WREN: Halo is breaking formation.
- 50 % — HALO: Ember — whatever happens, you get them to that ga— `[Halo signal lost]`
- 52 % — WREN: … Halo's transponder is offline. I am sorry.
- 60 % — WREN: Silver rings restore shields. Fly through them.
- 82 % — SATO: Two heavy signatures closing. Wardens. Keep them off my hull.
- 96 % — SATO: We're clear of orbit. Good flying, Ember. Halo would have said so.

- 4 % — WREN: Ice density is high. Recommend not hitting it.

- 40 % — WREN: Guard supply beacon ahead. Someone left us a present.
